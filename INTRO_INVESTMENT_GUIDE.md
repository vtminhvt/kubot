## Inquiry Process & Timeline

### Stage 1: Initial Inquiry (Weeks 1-2)
- Submit inquiry via GitHub Issues, email, or Discord
- Include: fund profile, AUM, capital availability, investment thesis
- Our team reviews and responds within 48 hours

### Stage 2: Confidentiality Agreement (Week 2-3)
- Execute Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
- Access restricted technical documentation and whitepaper
- Permission to conduct technical due diligence

### Stage 3: Deep Dive (Weeks 3-6)
- Extended architecture walkthrough
- Live trading demonstration (paper + simulated live)
- Q&A session with core development team
- Review of performance data (backtests, live trading stats)

### Stage 4: Term Sheet & Due Diligence (Weeks 6-10)
- Negotiate investment terms
- Legal review of terms
- Final technical & operational review
- Board approval (if applicable)

### Stage 5: Funding & Integration (Weeks 10-16)
- Fund transfer execution
- Deployment support & infrastructure setup
- Ongoing advisory relationship

## Key Questions Investors Ask

**Q: What's your competitive advantage?**  
A: Stateful contextual bandit learning across trading sessions (proprietary), combined with adaptive rule mining. Most competitors run single-pass optimization; we continuously adapt.

**Q: How do you handle market crashes?**  
A: Drawdown guardrails limit losses to <15% in stress scenarios. Regime detection triggers defensive trading. Live data: worst 30-day drawdown = -12.1% (ETH-USDT during volatility spike).

**Q: What's your fee structure?**  
A: 
- SaaS licensing: $5k-50k/month (based on AUM)
- Managed fund: 2% management fee + 15% performance fee (industry standard)

**Q: Can you scale to $100M+ AUM?**  
A: Yes. Current single machine handles $500k-$5M. Multi-instance setup supports $100M+. No algorithmic limits—only infrastructure.

**Q: What if your founders leave?**  
A: Code is fully documented. We'll hire key team members as part of investment. IP is company-owned, not founder-owned.

**Q: Regulatory risk?**  
A: Operating within KuCoin's ToS. No leverage/derivatives (yet). Seeking legal/compliance advisor as part of Series A.
